# Podcast Visualizer 1.3.3

Status: released on 2026-09-23 from signed tag `v1.3.3`, source commit
`5d5a026f6e68a7631d01e92478b2681f1afb0e3a`, and build 26.
[Download the notarized Apple Silicon DMG](https://github.com/aindaco1/podcast-visualizer/releases/download/v1.3.3/Podcast-Visualizer-1.3.3-arm64.dmg)
or view [the public release](https://github.com/aindaco1/podcast-visualizer/releases/tag/v1.3.3).

Native transcript approval preserves complete source sentence boundaries,
including questions and closing quotations. Apple's local sentence tokenizer
keeps abbreviations attached to their continuation. Unfinished phrases receive
optional Apple Intelligence advice in batches of six; incomplete or invalid
responses fall back safely. Words, acoustic speaker authority, timing limits,
review edits, and immutable revisions remain protected.

Speech and Apple generation infrastructure now come from the shared
Dust Wave Platform native package. Product policy stays in Podcast Visualizer.
FluidAudio remains pinned to 0.15.5; this release does not claim a new ASR model
or improved recognition accuracy. New runtime provenance binds the Platform
revision while validating older Record-backed runtimes.

Developer-only Jev evaluation uses hash-allowlisted synthetic text. Real audio,
transcripts, and review data stay on the Mac. The app contains no Jev provider
or credentials. See [the evaluation guide](../testing/jev-evaluation.md) and
[shared migration contract](../shared-native-migration.md).
