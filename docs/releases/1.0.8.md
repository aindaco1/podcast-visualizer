# Podcast Visualizer 1.0.8

Podcast Visualizer 1.0.8 makes rendered dialogue substantially easier to read
while preserving the approved transcript and exact forced-alignment evidence.
It also adds an early-warning CI path for macOS 27 without changing current
macOS 15 compatibility.

## More readable video dialogue

The renderer now measures the bundled Inter font instead of estimating lines
from character counts. It creates aspect-aware visual cues of at most two lines,
prefers punctuation and real acoustic pauses, avoids weak function-word breaks
and one-word tails, and never crosses a speaker change or long pause. Dialogue
stays in a stable upper-left reading area with only a subtle speaker-dependent
shift and a translucent contrast plate.

All reviewed punctuation—including commas, colons, semicolons, em dashes,
quotation marks, exclamation points, question marks, and ellipses—now informs
line and cue boundaries. A conservative display-only pass can add commas around
acoustically parenthetical discourse markers, a comma for emphatic repetition,
or an em dash for an immediate same-speaker restart. It does not use Parakeet
as an LLM and does not delete, replace, reorder, retime, or reassign words.

Every scene now includes a hash-bound readability report, also written beside
the scene as JSON, with source/visible word evidence, display-only punctuation
operations, measured line widths, reading speed, and flagged short or overlong
cues. Existing projects and renders remain immutable.

## macOS 27 readiness

Stable Swift tests and Apple Silicon builds remain pinned to macOS 15 with
Xcode 26.3. A separate non-blocking Xcode 27 preview lane detects SDK/compiler
regressions early, with a documented triage and promotion procedure. The app
does not preemptively change its deployment target, sandbox, hardened runtime,
or entitlements.

## Privacy and distribution

All transcript, media, alignment, and rendering work remains local to the Mac.
The app is self-contained for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later.
The GitHub release includes the signed and notarized archive and DMG, signed
Sparkle metadata and delta update, checksums, SBOM, build metadata,
notarization evidence, artifact-size evidence, and build provenance.
